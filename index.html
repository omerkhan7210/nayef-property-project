SEO scope

What this phase covers, and what it deliberately doesn't.

Technical SEO is built into the site from day one. Full SEO, the kind that involves ongoing strategy and content, is a different engagement with a different skillset, and I'd rather say that plainly than blur it into this quote.

Included in this build

  • Schema markup per page type (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Article)
  • Editable meta titles and descriptions on every page, no developer needed
  • Clean URL structure and internal linking between services and related guides
  • XML sitemap, robots.txt, and Rich Results validation before launch
  • Mobile speed optimization and server-rendered content for crawler access

Out of scope, separate engagement

  • Keyword research and content strategy
  • Writing or commissioning the guides and articles themselves
  • Link building or outreach
  • Monthly ranking reports or ongoing optimization
  • Paid search or ad campaign management

Questions worth answering now

What you're likely already wondering.

Anticipated rather than waiting for the call, so we can spend that time on decisions instead of logistics.

Why WordPress instead of a custom-coded or headless build?

Headless buys you speed you don't need at this scale and costs you the "no developer, no deploy step" requirement you specifically asked for. WordPress with server-rendered pages gets you the crawler-readability and mobile speed you need, while keeping every edit inside a dashboard you control.

Can I really edit everything myself, including titles and meta descriptions?

Yes. RankMath puts a title and description field directly on every page and article's edit screen. No code, no developer, no deploy step, exactly as specified in your brief.

What happens if I want to add a new service category after launch?

A new service page follows the same template as the six built in Phase 2, so you or I can add one without touching the underlying structure. If you want it built for you post-launch, that's a small hourly task, not a re-scope.

If I bring in a different developer later, is the site portable?

Yes. WordPress with Elementor is one of the most widely supported combinations available, and since code, hosting and domain all sit in your accounts from day one, any developer can pick this up without needing anything from me.

What if a phase runs into revisions beyond what's included?

One structured revision round is built into each phase's price. Anything beyond that gets scoped and quoted as a small add-on before I start it, never billed after the fact without your sign-off.

Does structured data actually guarantee AI or rich-result visibility?

No, and I wouldn't want to imply that. Schema makes you eligible for rich results and easier for AI systems to parse accurately. Whether you rank or get cited depends on content quality and competition, which is why full SEO is scoped separately.

What does the resources library cost me to maintain long-term?

Nothing beyond hosting and the plugin licenses already listed. Publishing a new article is the same as writing a page in any CMS, no per-article cost, no plugin limit on volume.

What I'd want to walk through on the call.

The taxonomy and CPT structure for the resources library, specifically how it holds up once listings arrive in a later phase. That's the one decision worth getting right before anything else gets built.
