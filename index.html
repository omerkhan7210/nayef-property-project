Built for what comes next

The resources system, not just a blog.

This is the piece worth getting right before build starts, since it's what a later listings integration will plug into.

Taxonomy

Categories and tags shared across the library, so an article about tenancy law and one about maintenance costs can both surface under the right filters.

Article template

One reusable layout with SEO fields, schema, and related-content built in, rather than a one-off design per article.

Archive

Filterable, paginated, built to stay fast at a few hundred entries rather than a few dozen.

If property listings arrive in a later phase, they attach to this same content model, categories, taxonomies, and templates, rather than requiring a rebuild of what's already live and indexed.
